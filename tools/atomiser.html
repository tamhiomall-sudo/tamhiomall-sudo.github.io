Atomiser

Take text you already wrote, mark where each piece should break, and export the parts straight into the Blocks tool as drafts. Everything runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded.

1. Text & break marks

Drop a .txt, .md or .docx file here, or click to choose. Or paste below.
A line starting with the marker begins a new atom. The rest of that line becomes its title. Tick the box if your source already uses # headings, and the marker switches to @@ so your real headings are left alone.

2. Block defaults

Applied to every atom. Order auto-numbers from 1. Titles come from your marker lines and you can edit them below.

3. Anonymise (mechanical pass)

Strip replaces each term everywhere across the atoms. With => client it becomes {{client}}, matching your block placeholders. With no arrow it becomes [redacted]. Scan only flags suspects (names, numbers with units, postcodes): it never removes. The judgement call stays yours. This is a safety net, not the full stranger test.
